## Verdict

- **Validity:** Valid with these fixes. Only the naturalistic rivals to theism (L1, computable L2(a)) are constructed. Theistic and non-deceiving rivals are asserted and need B3: P3 is *a priori*, while epistemic possibility is record-relative. "Only place" is false.
- **C-a "other substantive":** Invalid as written (counterexample above). It becomes valid but definitional once D9 is amended.
- **Soundness:** Conditional on P1–P3 and B1–B3, each owned with a refutation condition. B3 is the weakest. The problem of evil is fought there, so it cannot stay on the cut list.
- **Sufficiency:** With B3 and these edits, the items suffice for C-a, C-b, C-c and parity. Without B3, parity fails: one side is constructed, the other rests on a premise that does not reach the record.
