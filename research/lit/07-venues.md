# 07 — Venues: author guidelines (checked 2026-11-01)

All pages were accessed on 2026-11-01 unless a Wayback snapshot date is given. "Unverified" means I could not reach a primary page.

## Per-venue facts

**Religious Studies (CUP).** Source: cambridge.org/core/journals/religious-studies/information/author-instructions/preparing-your-materials, plus /fees-and-pricing and /peer-review-information/review-process.
- Length: "should not normally be longer than 8000 words." Abstract: at most 250 words, with 5–10 keywords.
- Review is double-anonymized.
- Unaffiliated authors: not stated.
- Fees: research articles are all Gold OA, with an APC of **£2610 / $3655**. Waivers can be requested after acceptance, and decisions are made "without reference to the funding situation."
- Preprints are not treated as prior publication.
- AI: AI cannot be an author. Generative use, including translation, must be declared. Minimal, non-generative use does not need declaring.
- Turnaround: unverified.

**Faith and Philosophy (SCP; PDC / Asbury).** The sources conflict.
- pdcnet.org/faithphil/Submission-Guidelines names editor Senor and asks for 3,000–10,000 words, a 100-word abstract and anonymous review.
- The live journal host (faithandphilosophy.com, which redirects to place.asburyseminary.edu/faithandphilosophy/) names editor Kvanvig and takes submissions by Google Form. The PDC page is probably stale.
- The journal is OA. Fees, AI policy, preprint policy, affiliation rules and turnaround are all unverified (not stated).

**International Journal for Philosophy of Religion (Springer).** Source: link.springer.com/journal/11153/submission-guidelines.
- Word limit: none stated. Abstract: 150–250 words.
- Review is double-blind, and the author must anonymize the manuscript.
- **Unaffiliated authors are explicitly accommodated**: "For authors that are (temporarily) unaffiliated we will only capture their city and country of residence."
- The journal is hybrid, so OA is optional. The APC amount is unverified.
- AI: this is the standard Springer Nature wording, a publisher-wide policy. LLMs "do not currently satisfy our authorship criteria"; LLM use must be "documented in the Methods section" or another suitable section; AI copy-editing needs no declaration.
- Preprints: not addressed on the page (unverified).
- Median submission to first decision is **17 days**, from the journal homepage metrics.

**Sophia (Springer).** Sources: the submission guidelines page (2026-11-01) and the homepage metrics (Wayback 2026-01-07).
- Articles are **3,000–8,000 words including references and notes**. Abstract: 150–250 words.
- Review is double-blind. Unaffiliated authors get the same clause as IJPR.
- The AI rules are the same Springer wording as IJPR. The journal is hybrid. Preprint policy is not stated.
- Median submission to first decision is **126 days**.

**European Journal for Philosophy of Religion — AVOID; treat as compromised (unverified).**
- The domain registered with its DOIs, philosophy-of-religion.eu, now shows clear red flags: off-topic papers (e.g. on bank talent management), conflicting statements about how often it publishes, and dead indexing logos.
- Crossref shows papers deposited in 2023 under the EJPR prefix 10.24204 that have nothing to do with the field, e.g. "…Marxist Philosophy in [Modern Dance] Creation" (10.24204/ejpr.2021.3876). Crossref lists 0 current DOIs.
- The journal has no DOAJ record. I could not find a legitimate current home, so I could not verify any guidelines.

**Religions (MDPI).** Sources: Wayback snapshots of /instructions (2026-09-24), /apc (2026-09-20) and the homepage (2026-10-02); DOAJ.
- Abstract: "about 200 words maximum". No hard length limit was found.
- Review is double-anonymized.
- APC is **CHF 1800**.
- Preprints are accepted, with Preprints.org integration.
- AI: GenAI use must be disclosed in Methods or Acknowledgements, naming the tool and version.
- Median first decision is **26.7 days** (H1 2026).
- Affiliations are expected in the "PubMed format".

**Philosophia Christi (EPS).** Source: epsociety.org/guidelines/ and /submit-an-article/.
- Articles: **4,000–9,000 words**. Notes: at most 4,000 words. No abstract is required.
- Review is blind, and the author must anonymize footnotes.
- Affiliation is optional ("Institution (optional)"), but "specialists and established scholars are preferred."
- No statement of faith is required, and contributors need not be EPS members.
- No fees are stated. Authors may post the "non-formatted version… as submitted."
- AI policy and turnaround: none stated.

**Zygon (Open Library of Humanities).** Source: DOAJ record (updated 2026-09-17); zygonjournal.org itself is behind an Anubis bot wall.
- **No APC** (diamond OA), CC BY licence, double-anonymous review.
- DOAJ gives about 23 weeks to publication.
- Word limits, AI policy and affiliation rules are unverified. Read zygonjournal.org/site/author-guidelines/ in a browser.

**Theology and Science (T&F).** Source: author instructions, Wayback 2026-05-12.
- "Typical paper… between 5000 and 8000 words." Abstract: an unstructured 100 words.
- Review is **single-anonymous by one reviewer**. T&F warns that anonymity "cannot be guaranteed" if the paper is on a preprint server.
- OA is optional through Open Select (APC via the APC finder). The AI policy is publisher-wide; I could not retrieve the page (unverified).

## AI bans (2025–2026)

Searching Daily Nous for 2025–26 turned up **no philosophy-of-religion journal banning AI-written papers**. The relevant items:
- *Ethics* announced a policy (dailynous.com/2025/12/03/ethics-announces-ai-policy/). AI "cannot author or even co-author"; taking AI sentences as your own is plagiarism; use must be disclosed; AI-originated content "may be deemed less desirable."
- A referee described receiving an LLM-written paper with hallucinated references (2025/10/28).
- Schwitzgebel argued that journals *should* reject AI-written submissions (2026/07/16).

Practical upshot: every journal checked allows disclosed assistance; none allows AI authorship. Expect referees to be sceptical of a heavily AI-drafted paper. The disclosure should say the author did the argumentation and checked every citation by hand.

## Preprint servers

- **PhilArchive** (FAQ, Wayback 2026-10-02) accepts "all areas of academic philosophy, so long as they are of professional quality." It is moderated by the PhilPapers team, free, and has a Philosophy of Religion category. **Suitable.** No AI-content policy was found (unverified).
- **PhilSci-Archive**: the site blocked me (unverified). Its scope is philosophy of science, so it is a fit only if the paper is framed as formal epistemology or underdetermination.

## Recommendation (8–10k words, philosophical prose, independent author, disclosed AI)

1. **IJPR.** No stated word cap, explicit provision for unaffiliated authors, no mandatory fee, a clear Springer disclosure route, and a 17-day median first decision.
2. **Faith and Philosophy.** Allows up to 10k words, OA, with no fee found. Confirm the current limits and AI stance with the editor first.
3. **Religious Studies.** The highest-profile option, but it needs cutting to about 8k words, and the £2610 APC requires a waiver request. **Zygon** is the fallback: diamond OA, and it suits the formal/science framing.

Avoid EJPR (compromised) and Sophia (8k cap including references, 126-day median first decision).
