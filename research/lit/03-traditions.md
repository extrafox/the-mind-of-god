# Lit strand 03: How traditions say one comes to know God

**V** means the quote was checked at the source listed at the end. **U** means unverified, so check it before citing.

## 1. Catholic

- **Natural knowledge.** *Dei Filius* ch. 2: God "can be known with certainty from the consideration of created things, by the natural power of human reason" (V). Canon 2.1 anathematizes anyone who denies this (V). This is a claim about a *capacity*. CCC 37 concedes "many difficulties in coming to know God by the light of reason alone", so revelation is needed (CCC 38) (V). CCC 31 calls the proofs "converging and convincing arguments", "not in the sense of proofs in the natural sciences" (V).
- **Faith.** *Dei Filius* ch. 3: we believe "not because we perceive its intrinsic truth by the natural light of reason, but because of the authority of God himself" (V). Miracles and prophecies are "the most certain signs of revelation", so assent "is by no means a blind movement of the mind" (V). These are the "motives of credibility" (CCC 156, V). Yet no one accepts the gospel "without the inspiration and illumination of the holy Spirit" (V).
- **Canons.** Canon 3.3 rejects faith moved "only by each one's internal experience or private inspiration" (V). Canon 3.6 forbids Catholics to suspend assent "until they have completed a scientific demonstration" (V).
- **Aquinas.** Believing is "an act of the intellect assenting to the Divine truth at the command of the will moved by the grace of God" (ST II-II q.2 a.9, V). ST II-II q.4 a.8: by its cause, "faith is more certain, because it is founded on the Divine truth"; by the subject, "faith is less certain, because matters of faith are above the human intellect"; so "faith is more certain simply, while the others are more certain relatively, i.e. for us" (V).
- **CCC 157.** Faith is "more certain than all human knowledge because it is founded on the very word of God who cannot lie". It quotes Newman: "Ten thousand difficulties do not make one doubt" (V).

**Claims:** natural knowledge (in principle), and faith whose certitude exceeds science's. **Routes:** 1, 2, 5. Route 3 is rejected as a sole basis.

**Misfit:** Aquinas's certainty "by its cause" is *objective and source-based*. It is not psychological firmness. Canon 3.6 is a norm, not a route.

## 2. Orthodox

- **Pseudo-Dionysius.** Moses "enters into the gloom of the Agnosia … by knowing nothing, knowing above mind" (*Mystical Theology* 1, Parker trans., V). The Cause is "above every definition … above every abstraction" (ch. 5, V).
- **Evagrius.** "If you are a theologian, you truly pray. If you truly pray, you are a theologian" (V; *On Prayer* 60 in Bamberger's numbering, 61 in the Philokalia's).
- **Palamas.** God's essence is unknowable; the uncreated energies are participable (the light of Tabor). No verified quote (U); use Meyendorff, *The Triads* (1983).
- **Lossky.** *Mystical Theology of the Eastern Church* (French 1944; English 1957) is verified. Two attributed phrases are both U and must be checked against ch. 1: "an attitude of mind which refuses to form concepts about God", and "no theology without mysticism".

**Claims:** knowledge beyond concepts; participation (*theosis*). **Routes:** 3, framed by 2.

**Misfit:** this knowledge is not propositional. It does not take the form "belief that a worldview is true".

## 3. Protestant

- **Calvin, sensus divinitatis.** "there exists in the human minds and indeed by natural instinct, some sense of Deity" (*Inst.* I.3.1, Beveridge trans., V).
- **Calvin, testimony of the Spirit.** "the testimony of the Spirit is superior to reason". Scripture "deigns not to submit to proofs and arguments", and "the only true faith is that which the Spirit of God seals on our hearts" (I.7.4–5, V).
- **Barth.** He rejected natural theology. His reply to Brunner was "a book titled simply 'Nein!'" (1934) (V, SEP). The *analogia entis* critique has no verified quote (U).
- **Reformed epistemology.** "religious belief can be rational without any appeal to evidence or argument" (V, IEP). Plantinga holds that "there is no way to show that religious belief is not warranted without first assuming that it is false" (V). Great Pumpkin objections: IEP (V). Source: Plantinga, *Warranted Christian Belief* (2000).

**Claims:** "full conviction" (Calvin); externalist warrant (Plantinga). **Routes:** 4; 5 and 3 (the Spirit's testimony); 2. Route 1 is refused as a foundation.

**Misfits:** Scripture is self-authenticating, which is circular by design. Barth's view of revelation as God's act is the strongest form of P2\*.

## 4. Islamic

- **Fitrah.** Q 30:30: "the fiṭrah of Allāh upon which He has created [all] people" (Sahih Intl., V). The hadith is in Muslim 2658 and Bukhari 1385 (V).
- **Kalam.** Al-Ghazali, *Iqtiṣād*: "Every being which begins has a cause for its beginning; now the world is a being which begins" (V *as quoted by* Craig). Craig takes it second-hand from de Beaurecueil 1947, so check it against a translation of the *Iqtiṣād*.
- **Munqidh** (Watt trans.):
  - Certain knowledge is that "in which the object is disclosed in such a fashion that no doubt remains along with it" (V).
  - His skepticism was cured "by a light which God most high cast into my breast" (V).
  - Sufism is known by "immediate experience (dhawq—literally 'tasting')", as being drunk differs from knowing "the definition of drunkenness" (V).
  - The glosses *maʿrifa* (experiential knowledge) and *kashf* (unveiling) are U; check EI2.
- **Yaqīn.** *ʿIlm al-yaqīn* (Q 102:5), *ʿayn al-yaqīn* (102:7), *ḥaqq al-yaqīn* (56:95; 69:51) (V). Reading these as an ascending ladder is a later Sufi reading (U).
- **Īmān.** *Taṣdīq* (assent). Whether *taqlīd* (faith by imitation) suffices was disputed (U as to detail). For later Ashʿarīs and Māturīdīs, accepting the Qur'an "is different than merely 'believing by faith' (taqlīd)" (V, SEP). Source: Frank, "Knowledge and Taqlîd" (*JAOS* 1989).
- **Istikhāra.** Bukhari 6382: the prayer *astakhīruka bi-ʿilmika*, "I ask You to choose for me by Your knowledge" (V). Q 5:3 forbids *istiqsām bi-l-azlām*, drawing lots for decisions (V).

**Claims:** *yaqīn* (certainty) in grades. **Routes:** 1, 2, 3, 4, 5.

**Misfits:**
- (a) **Istikhāra is not a chance method.** It is supplication.
- (b) Ghazali's "light" restores trust in reason itself. It is a grace-route that grounds the other routes.
- (c) The *taqlīd* debate is a dispute about a §4B standard.

## 5. Jewish

- **Maimonides, Guide** (Friedlander trans.). "the negative attributes of God are the true attributes" (I.58, V). "by each additional negative attribute you advance toward the knowledge of God" (I.59, V). I.59 cites "Silence is praise to Thee" (Ps 65:2) (V).
- **Maimonides, Mishneh Torah** (Touger trans.). Knowing "that there is a Primary Being" is "a positive commandment" (Yesodei haTorah 1:1, 1:6, V). Israel believed in Moses not "because of the wonders" but because of Sinai: "Our eyes saw, and not a stranger's" (8:1, V).
- **Halevi, Kuzari** (Hirschfeld numbering; Sefaria's numbers run 6 higher).
  - The creed is "the God of Abraham … who led the children of Israel out of Egypt" (I.11, V).
  - Speculative religion is "open to many doubts" (I.13, V).
  - The witnesses must be "great multitudes … [who] did not learn it from reports" (I.8, V).
  - The event "cannot be denied" (I.88, V).
  - The term "Kuzari argument" (Gottlieb, *The Informed Soul*, 1990) is U in scholarly use, and no published critique was verified.
- **Urim and Thummim.** Exod 28:30; Num 27:21; 1 Sam 28:6 (V). Yoma 73b: the letters "protrude" or "rearrange themselves" (V). The Urim ceased with the early prophets (m. Sotah 9:12; b. Sotah 48b) (V). Prov 16:33: "The lot is cast into the lap; but the whole of its decision is from the Lord" (V).

**Claims:** knowledge of God is commanded and demonstrable, but its content is negative. Halevi claims an "irrefutable" historical certainty. **Routes:** 1, 2, 3; oracle and lot.

**Misfits:**
- (a) Halevi is a 1+2 hybrid: an *inference* from features of a testimony.
- (b) The tradition reads the lot and the Urim as **oracles**, not chance.
- (c) The Urim is a route the tradition says has *ceased*.

## 6. Hindu

- **Nyāya.** "The four *pramāṇas* are perception, inference, analogical reasoning, and testimony" (V, IEP). The usual reference, NS 1.1.3, is U. NS 1.1.7: "Testimony is the (true) statement of an expert (āpta)" (V). Udayana: "Things like the earth have a maker as their cause, because they are products" (V, IEP).
- **Mīmāṃsā.** Knowledge is "self-certifying, svataḥ prāmāṇya". The Veda is "apauruṣeya" (authorless). "Mīmāṃsā, however, is atheistic" (V, SEP).
- **Advaita.** Brahman "can be known only through the scriptures" (BS 1.1.3), for "reasoning has no sure basis" (BS 2.1.11) (Vireswarananda trans., V). Śaṅkara "accepts nonduality based on the Upaniṣad's authority alone" (V, SEP). The SEP denies that this knowledge is "an experiential state" (V).

**Claims:** *pramā* (knowledge), not faith. **Routes:** 1 (Nyāya), 2.

**Misfits:**
- (a) *Svataḥ prāmāṇya* is a §4B **standard** that §4B lacks (default validity unless defeated).
- (b) The scripture has no divine author.
- (c) In Advaita the knower and Brahman are not two. *Jñāna* removes ignorance rather than adding a belief.

## 7. Buddhist (contrast)

Dharmakīrti: "only two … 'sources of knowledge' (pramāṇa) … 'perception' … and 'inference'". Scripture is "an inferential source of knowledge". He argues against "the permanent creator God (īśvara)" (V, SEP). That this is in the *Pramāṇavārttika* chapter is U.

**Contrast:** a non-theist worldview that folds route 2 into route 1. It is still covered by §0.

## Recommendations

1. **Move istikhāra out of "routes that use chance".** It is a supplication, and Q 5:3 forbids lot-divination. For the lot and the Urim, say that the tradition reads the device as an oracle (Prov 16:33). AA's randomized C2 covers the device; P2\* covers the claimed divine steering.
2. **Rebuild certitude versus certainty around ST II-II q.4 a.8.** Certainty "by cause" is an externalist claim (§4B, standard 2; C2 and lemma L). "Less certain … for us" is Aquinas conceding the paper's point. This is stronger than "psychological certitude".
3. **Add *svataḥ prāmāṇya* to §4B**, alongside credulity.
4. **Add a hybrid note to §4A** (Calvin 3+5→2; Halevi 1+2; Ghazali 5→1). Because the grid answers by standard, hybrids leave no gap.
5. **Address non-propositional knowledge** (apophatic, Advaita, *maʿrifa*) in §6.1 or §6.4. The paper targets the doctrinal claims that the experience is said to warrant.
6. **Use Vatican I as the explicit opponent of C1** (canons 2.1 and 3.4). Note that it claims a capacity and that CCC 37 concedes the actual difficulty.
7. **Verify before citing:** Lossky; Palamas; McCarthy's *Munqidh*; EI2 "Īmān", "Maʿrifa", "Kas̲h̲f" and "Istik̲h̲āra"; the *Iqtiṣād* wording; NS 1.1.3; critics of the "Kuzari argument"; Barth, *CD* II/1.
